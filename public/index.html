Features

Everything you need to
fix campus problems

From reporting to resolution — FixSpot handles the full workflow so nothing slips through the cracks.

📸
Photo Evidence
Attach a photo directly from your camera or gallery. A picture is worth a thousand words — and faster fixes.
📍
GPS Location
Your exact coordinates are automatically attached to each report so maintenance knows exactly where to go.
🔔
Notifications
Get notified when your report status changes — from pending to in-progress to resolved. Always in the loop.
📊
Status Tracking
Follow every stage of your report with a clear timeline: Pending → In Progress → Resolved.
🛠️
Admin Dashboard
Maintenance staff get a powerful dashboard to manage, prioritize, and close reports with completion photos.
✏️
Edit & Delete
Made a mistake? Pending reports can be edited or deleted before the team picks them up.
How it works

Fixed in 4 simple steps

No paperwork, no waiting rooms, no phone calls. Just open FixSpot and report.

1
Sign Up
Create your account with your university email in under a minute.
2
Spot an Issue
Find a broken light, leaking pipe, or any campus problem worth fixing.
3
Submit a Report
Take a photo, let GPS pin the location, pick a category, and hit submit.
4
Track & Done
Get notified as your issue moves from pending to resolved — with a completion photo.

Privacy Policy

Your data,
your rights.

FixSpot is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Data We Collect

We only collect what is strictly necessary to operate the service.

  • Email address — for account authentication only. Never shared publicly.
  • Full name — displayed on your reports to identify the submitter.
  • GPS location — recorded only at the moment you submit a report. We do not track your location continuously.
  • Photos — uploaded images are stored securely on Cloudinary servers for the purpose of documenting issues.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — handles authentication and database storage.
  • Cloudinary — stores report images. Images are linked to your report only.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Resolved reports are kept for audit purposes for a maximum of 1 year, then deleted. You may request early deletion at any time.

Your Rights under PDPA

Right to Access

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure

Delete your account and all associated data at any time from your profile settings.

Right to Rectification

Update your name or contact information from within the app.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Stop using the service and request data removal by contacting us.

Security Measures

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Firebase Security Rules enforce role-based access control
  • Passwords are never stored — managed by Firebase Authentication
  • Admin access is strictly controlled and audited

Last updated: January 2025
For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, please contact us at the email below. We will respond within 30 days.
